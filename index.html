How do I arrange an appointment?

Use Nuffield's appointment booking page , contact a listed clinic, or call +65 6833 4353 for help choosing the next step.

Can I speak with someone before choosing a treatment?

Yes. Start with the general enquiry options or contact a clinic directly. You can describe your concern and ask which type of appointment to arrange.

Where can I check opening hours?

Follow a clinic's information link above or use the full clinic directory . Opening hours, public holiday arrangements and appointment availability can differ between branches.

Can I check costs before making a decision?

Review the published price guide . Your clinic can explain the proposed treatment, estimate and any applicable scheme requirements after assessment.
