# Nuffield Dental — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Nuffield Dental is a multidisciplinary dental group founded in Singapore in 2014. Its clinics provide routine care, implants, restorative dentistry, orthodontics and oral surgery, including care for nervous patients.

The website helps patients explore treatments, find a clinic and make enquiries. The redesigned homepage prioritises current clinic links, consistent clinic selection and direct contact options before the full booking process.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Current Clinic Links

Live clinic links replace the archived directory destination previously linked from the hero’s “dental clinic near me” text. Visitors now have a current route to clinic information from the opening section.

### 2. Consistent Clinic Selection

One consistent selector presents clinic choices with published addresses, telephone numbers and WhatsApp contacts. This addresses differences in names and options between the previous directory and enquiry form, helping patients identify and contact their preferred clinic.

### 3. Easier Initial Enquiries

Call, WhatsApp and email options are clearly available before the full booking process. Previously, the form requested treatment, date and time details before visitors might know what they needed. Patients can now seek guidance before providing those booking details.

## Design Improvements

- **Navigation:** The hero provides live links to clinic information.
- **Layout and consistency:** A single selector presents clinic choices and published contact details consistently.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Direct contact options are clearly available before the full booking process.
- **User experience:** Patients can choose a clinic and contact channel without first deciding on a treatment or appointment time.
